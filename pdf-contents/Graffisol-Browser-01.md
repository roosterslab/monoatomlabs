**How It Works ?**

Graffisol utilises ultra-fine graphene
nano structures uniformly dispersed
within a transparent coating matrix.
These enable:

Anti-reflective behaviour by
reducing surface light losses and
increasing photon absorption

Enhanced thermal dissipation,
lowering panel operating
temperature

Hydrophobic and dust-repellent
surface, minimizing soiling losses
