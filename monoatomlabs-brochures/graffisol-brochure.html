How It Works ?

Graffisol utilises ultra-fine graphene nano structures uniformly dispersed within a transparent coating matrix. These enable:

  • Anti-reflective behaviour by reducing surface light losses & increasing photon absorption
  • Enhanced thermal dissipation, lowering panel operating temp
  • Hydrophobic and dust-repellent surface, minimizing soiling losses
