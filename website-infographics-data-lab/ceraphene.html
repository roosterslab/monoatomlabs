Formula 1: Immediate Price Savings

Simple cost comparison per application

Source: Product brochure 2024
Competitor Average

Calculation

Savings per application = Pcompetitor − Pceraphene
S = ₹15,000 − ₹5,000 = ₹10,000
Savings percentage = (S / Pcompetitor) × 100
S% = (₹10,000 / ₹15,000) × 100 = 66.7%

Formula 2: Cost Per Protection-Year

Annual cost of protection (normalized by durability)

✓ Source-Backed Values

Ceraphene Durability
Yc = 3 to 4 years
Source: Field validation reports

Calculation

Cost per year = Price / Durability (years)
If Yc = 3 years:
CPY = ₹5,000 / 3 = ₹1,666/year
If Yc = 4 years:
CPY = ₹5,000 / 4 = ₹1,250/year

💡 Key Insight

Ceraphene costs ₹1,250-₹1,666 per year of protection. This metric helps customers compare different products with different lifespans on an apples-to-apples basis.
