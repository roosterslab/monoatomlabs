## 📊 A/B TESTING RECOMMENDATIONS

### **Primary Tests:**

**Test 1: Problem vs. Outcome**
- Variation A (Problem-Agitation) vs. Variation B (Outcome-First)
- Measures: Click-through, time on page, bounce rate
- Hypothesis: Outcome-first performs better for commercial traffic

**Test 2: Vision vs. Credibility**
- Variation C (Vision-Led) vs. Variation D (Credibility-First)
- Measures: Brand perception, engagement depth

## 💡 COPYWRITING TIPS

### **Headlines:**
- Keep under 10 words for maximum impact
- Use numbers when possible (40%, 200+, 4 products)
- Lead with benefit or provocative statement
- Avoid jargon unless targeting technical audience

### **Subheadlines:**
- Expand on headline (don't repeat)
- Add credibility or specificity
- 15-25 words ideal length
- Can be sentence or fragment

### **Supporting Copy:**
- Front-load most important info
- Use bullet points for scannability
- Include proof points (metrics, certifications)
- Keep paragraphs to 2-3 sentences max

### **CTAs:**
- Action-oriented verbs (Explore, Calculate, See, Request)
- Specific outcomes (not just "Learn More")
- Primary CTA = high intent, Secondary = lower intent
- Contrast visually (color, size, placement)
