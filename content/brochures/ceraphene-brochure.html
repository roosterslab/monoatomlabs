How It Works ?

Ceraphene utilises graphene nano-platelets embedded within a ceramic coating matrix. Once applied, this structure enables:

  • Formation of a dense, chemically
    bonded protective layer
  • Enhanced resistance to heat, UV
    radiation, and oxidation
  • Reduced surface porosity, limiting
    dust, dirt, and contaminant
    adhesion
