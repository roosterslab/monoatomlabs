# 🧪 2. GRAPHENE / GRAPHACRETE COST (REALITY)

Graphene-based admixtures (like Graphacrete / Monoatom-style systems):

### Key facts:

* Dosage: **0.05% – 0.5% of cement weight** ([G-Hexa][1])
* Graphene improves:

  * Strength ↑ up to 50% ([GraphenePioneer][2])
  * Cement reduction up to 30% ([ConcreteGraphene][3])
* Typical graphene admixture cost:

  * ₹180/L (polymer graphene mix) ([Czartions][4])
  * ₹2,500/L (3% graphene solution) ([Graphene Industries][5])

👉 Graphene oxide can be **very expensive in lab scale**
(e.g. ₹25,000/kg) ([Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs][6])

# 🔥 5. REAL INDUSTRY INSIGHT (VERY IMPORTANT)

Graphene concrete is **NOT used to save per m³ cost directly**.

It is used to:

### 1. Reduce cement consumption

* Cement is biggest cost + CO₂ contributor

### 2. Increase durability

* Less repair cost

### 3. Reduce structural weight

* Smaller columns, less steel

### 4. Faster construction

* Faster curing (up to 25%) ([GraphenePioneer][2])

# 🧠 6. FINAL ENGINEERING CONCLUSION

### If your goal is **low-cost construction**

👉 Use:

* Fly ash + GGBS + superplasticizer
  👉 Cost: **₹200–800/m³**

---

### If your goal is **premium / infra / AI-wave / future materials**

👉 Use Graphene / Graphacrete

👉 Economics:

* Initial cost: **same or slightly higher**
* Lifecycle cost: **much lower**
* Carbon footprint: **lower**
