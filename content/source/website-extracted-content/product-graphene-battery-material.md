# Graphene Battery Material - Monoatomlabs

## Product Overview

Monoatomlabs offers graphene-enhanced battery materials designed to improve LFP (Lithium Iron Phosphate) cell performance without requiring changes to existing manufacturing processes.

## Key Features

The graphene anode and cathode materials enable batteries with:
- Ultra-fast charging capabilities
- Extended lifespan
- Enhanced thermal safety

## Technical Specifications

- **Purity:** ~99%
- **Thickness (Z):** ~5-10 nm
- **Dimension (X&Y):** ~20 µm
- **Surface Area:** 200 m²/g
- **Bulk Density:** 0.12 g/cm³
