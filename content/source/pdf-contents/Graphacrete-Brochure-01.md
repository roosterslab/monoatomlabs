**How It Works ?**

Graphacrete utilises graphene nano
platelets dispersed uniformly within the
cementitious matrix. These create:

A tortuous path for moisture
blocking water ingress.

Nano-reinforced microstructure –
resisting cracks and shrinkage.

Catalytic support for C-S-H gel
formation – reducing porosity and
boosting cohesion.
