- Marine and coastal structures
- Sustainable housing projects

**Competitive Advantage:**

- Best cost-to-performance ratio
- Strongest CO₂ reduction profile

**Technical Specs:**

- Hydrophobicity: Excellent
- Heat & UV resistance: Excellent
- Gloss & color depth: Excellent
- Cost effectiveness: Very high
- Maintenance frequency: Low

**Cost Advantage:**
- Price: ₹5,000/50ml
- Competitor average: ₹15,000/50ml
- 60-70% lower cost than premium ceramic coatings
- Lower cost per protection year
