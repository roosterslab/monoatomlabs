# Innovation Pipeline

Monoatom Labs engineers advanced material solutions that address real industrial challenges across multiple sectors including coating, mobility, defense, aerospace, water purification, energy storage, and more.

---

## Products in Pipeline

Our next-generation products currently under development:

### [Rustene](./rustene.md)
Graphene-based anti-rusting paint forms an ultra-thin barrier preventing corrosion and extending metal lifespan.

**Applications:** Industrial metal structures, marine equipment, automotive components, infrastructure protection

---

### [Graphyre](./graphyre.md)
Graphene-reinforced tyres delivering higher strength, lower rolling resistance, and extended service life.

**Applications:** Automotive tyres, commercial vehicles, heavy-duty industrial tyres, performance vehicles

---

### [Graphosite](./graphosite.md)
Lightweight, high-strength graphene-reinforced fibres engineered for next-generation structural composites.

**Applications:** Aerospace components, automotive structures, sports equipment, industrial composites

---

### [Thermaphene](./thermaphene.md)
Graphene-based heating solution enabling lightweight and high-performance winter wear.

**Applications:** Winter clothing, outdoor sports gear, military cold-weather equipment, wearable heating systems

---

### [Armophene](./armophene.md)
Graphene-infused bulletproof jacket delivering lightweight, flexible armor with superior ballistic protection compared to Kevlar.

**Applications:** Law enforcement body armor, military protective equipment, security personnel protection, defense applications

---

## Field-Scale Pilot Trials

Advanced technologies currently undergoing real-world testing and validation:

### [Hydrogen Separation Membranes](./hydrogen-separation-membranes.md)
High-selectivity graphene membranes enabling efficient hydrogen separation and production with reduced energy losses.

**Applications:** Hydrogen production facilities, clean energy infrastructure, fuel cell technology, renewable energy systems

---

### [Seawater Desalination Membranes](./seawater-desalination-membranes.md)
Ultra-thin graphene membranes delivering high-flux, low-energy seawater desalination and industrial water purification.

**Applications:** Seawater desalination plants, industrial water treatment, municipal water purification, coastal water supply systems

---

### [Atmospheric Moisture Harvesting](./atmospheric-moisture-harvesting.md)
Graphene membranes engineered to harvest atmospheric moisture and convert air into potable water efficiently.

**Applications:** Arid and desert regions, emergency water supply, off-grid communities, disaster relief operations

---

### [Graphene-Reinforced Glass Fibres](./graphene-glass-fibres.md)
Graphene-reinforced glass fibres offering enhanced strength, durability, and multifunctional performance in composites.

**Applications:** Composite manufacturing, construction materials, automotive components, marine structures

---

### [Battery Storage Systems](./battery-storage-systems.md)
Graphene-enabled battery storage systems delivering higher power density, faster response, and extended lifecycle.

**Applications:** Renewable energy storage, grid stabilization, electric vehicle batteries, backup power systems
