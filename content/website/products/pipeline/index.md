## RUSTENE - Anti-Corrosion Paint

### Overview
Graphene-based anti-rusting paint that forms an ultra-thin barrier preventing corrosion and extending metal lifespan.

### How It Works
Rustene utilizes graphene nano-platelets to create an impermeable barrier layer that blocks moisture, oxygen, and corrosive agents from reaching metal surfaces while maintaining flexibility and adhesion.

### Expected Benefits
- Superior corrosion resistance compared to traditional coatings
- Ultra-thin barrier prevents moisture and oxygen ingress
- Extended metal structure lifespan
- Excellent adhesion to various metal substrates
- Flexibility prevents cracking and peeling
- Environmentally friendly formulation

### Target Applications
- Industrial metal equipment and machinery
- Marine vessels and offshore structures
- Metal infrastructure (bridges, towers, railings)
- Automotive underbody protection
- Storage tanks and containers
- Coastal and high-humidity environments

### Target Markets
- Marine industry
- Infrastructure development
- Manufacturing and industrial sectors
- Automotive industry
- Oil and gas facilities

## GRAPHYRE - Tire Technology

### Overview
Graphene-reinforced tyres delivering higher strength, lower rolling resistance, and extended service life.

### How It Works
By incorporating graphene nano-platelets into tire rubber compounds, Graphyre enhances molecular bonding and creates a reinforced structure with superior mechanical properties and heat dissipation.

### Expected Benefits
- Higher tensile and tear strength
- Reduced rolling resistance for improved fuel efficiency
- Extended tire service life (30-50% improvement expected)
- Better heat dissipation during operation
- Improved wet and dry grip performance
- Enhanced durability under load

### Target Applications
- Passenger vehicle tires
- Commercial vehicle and truck tires
- Performance and racing tires
- Heavy equipment and construction tires
- Two-wheeler tires

### Target Markets
- Tire manufacturers
- Automotive OEMs
- Fleet operators
- Performance vehicle market

[Learn More About Graphyre](/products/pipeline/graphyre)

## GRAPHOSITE - Advanced Composites

### Overview
Lightweight, high-strength graphene-reinforced fibres engineered for next-generation structural composites.

### How It Works
Graphosite integrates graphene into fiber matrices to create composite materials with exceptional strength-to-weight ratios, enabling lighter yet stronger structural components.

### Expected Benefits
- Exceptional strength-to-weight ratio
- High structural integrity and load-bearing capacity
- Significant material weight reduction (20-40%)
- Enhanced durability and fatigue resistance
- Superior impact resistance
- Improved thermal and electrical properties

### Target Applications
- Aerospace structural components and fuselage parts
- Automotive body panels and chassis
- Sports equipment (bicycles, rackets, protective gear)
- Wind turbine blades
- Construction materials and prefab structures
- Marine hull construction

### Target Markets
- Aerospace and aviation
- Automotive industry
- Renewable energy (wind power)
- Sports equipment manufacturers
- Construction industry

## THERMAPHENE - Heating Solutions

### Overview
Graphene-based heating solution enabling lightweight and high-performance winter wear and heating applications.

### How It Works
Thermaphene leverages graphene's exceptional electrical conductivity to create ultra-thin, flexible heating elements that convert electricity to uniform heat with high efficiency.

### Expected Benefits
- Lightweight heating elements
- Uniform heat distribution across surfaces
- Energy efficient operation
- Flexible and washable for apparel applications
- Fast heating response time
- Low voltage operation for safety

### Target Applications
- High-performance winter clothing and jackets
- Automotive seat and steering wheel heating
- Building heating panels and flooring
- Wearable heated accessories
- Medical heating pads and therapeutic devices
- Industrial process heating

### Target Markets
- Apparel and outdoor gear manufacturers
- Automotive interior suppliers
- Building and construction
- Medical devices
- Consumer electronics

[Learn More About Thermaphene](/products/pipeline/thermaphene)

## ARMOPHENE - Protective Armor

### Overview
Graphene-infused bulletproof jacket delivering lightweight, flexible armor with superior ballistic protection compared to Kevlar.

### How It Works
Armophene incorporates graphene layers into protective fabric matrices, creating armor that disperses impact energy more effectively while maintaining flexibility and reducing weight.

### Expected Benefits
- Lighter than traditional Kevlar armor (30-50% weight reduction)
- More flexible for better mobility and comfort
- Superior ballistic protection and impact resistance
- Enhanced comfort for extended wear periods
- Better heat dissipation reduces wearer fatigue
- Multi-threat protection capability

### Target Applications
- Military body armor and tactical vests
- Law enforcement protective vests
- Security personnel protection
- VIP protection equipment
- Civilian personal protection
- Critical infrastructure security

### Target Markets
- Defense and military
- Law enforcement agencies
- Security services
- Government and VIP protection
- Civilian protection market
