## How Rustene Works

### Graphene-Based Barrier Technology

Rustene utilizes graphene nano-platelets dispersed within a specialized coating matrix to create a protective barrier with unprecedented impermeability.

#### The Science Behind Rustene

**1. Impermeable Barrier Formation**
- Graphene nano-platelets create overlapping layers
- Forms a tortuous path that blocks moisture and oxygen
- Prevents corrosive agents from reaching metal surface
- Ultra-thin yet highly effective protection

**2. Molecular Bonding**
- Strong adhesion to metal substrates
- Chemical bonding enhances durability
- Maintains flexibility to prevent cracking
- Resists peeling and delamination

**3. Multi-Layer Protection**
- First layer: Direct metal adhesion
- Middle layers: Graphene barrier structure
- Top layer: Environmental resistance
- Complete protection system

**4. Self-Healing Properties** (Expected)
- Graphene layers overlap and interlock
- Minor damage doesn't compromise entire barrier
- Continued protection even with surface scratches
- Extended service life

**Q: Can Rustene be applied over existing coatings?**
