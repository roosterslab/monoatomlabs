## How It Works

### Nano-Reinforcement Mechanism

Graphacrete utilizes graphene nano-platelets dispersed uniformly within the cementitious matrix. These create three critical performance enhancements:

#### Tortuous Path for Moisture
Graphene nano-platelets create a complex, tortuous path that blocks water ingress effectively, dramatically improving waterproofing and durability.

#### Nano-Reinforced Microstructure
The nano-platelets act as reinforcement at the microscopic level, resisting cracks and shrinkage while enhancing overall structural integrity.

#### Catalytic Support for C-S-H Gel Formation
Graphene surfaces support calcium-silicate-hydrate (C-S-H) gel formation, the primary binding phase in concrete, reducing porosity and boosting cohesion for stronger, denser concrete.
