## 2. Nanomaterial Functionalization

#### Functionalization Techniques

**Chemical Functionalization:**
- **Covalent Modification**
  - Carboxylation
  - Amination
  - Hydroxylation
  - Silane coupling
  - Polymer grafting

- **Non-Covalent Modification**
  - Surfactant wrapping
  - Polymer wrapping
  - π-π stacking
  - Electrostatic adsorption
  - Hydrogen bonding

**Physical Modification:**
- Particle size reduction
- Surface area optimization
- Aspect ratio control
- Morphology engineering

#### Application-Specific Functionalization

**Cementitious Materials (Graphacrete):**
- Hydrophilic surface modification
- Cement compatibility enhancement
- Dispersion stability in aqueous media
- Alkaline environment resistance

**Polymer Systems (HD-G-PE, Graphyre):**
- Polymer chain compatibility
- Melt processing stability
- Interfacial bonding enhancement
- Processing temperature optimization

**Coatings (Ceraphene, Graffisol, Rustene):**
- Solvent dispersion optimization
- Resin compatibility
- Surface adhesion improvement
- Film-forming property enhancement

**Energy Applications (Batteries, Solar):**
- Electrochemical performance optimization
- Conductivity enhancement
- Surface area maximization
- Stability in electrochemical environments

#### Characterization of Functionalization

**Analytical Methods:**
- FTIR (Fourier Transform Infrared Spectroscopy) - functional group identification
- XPS (X-ray Photoelectron Spectroscopy) - surface chemistry
- Raman Spectroscopy - structural changes
- TGA (Thermogravimetric Analysis) - functionalization degree
- Contact angle - surface energy changes
