How It Works?

A precisely engineered graphene-infused transparent layer modifies surface optical and thermal properties by:

  • Optimizing light transmission and reducing reflectance
  • Enhancing lateral heat dispersion across the panel surface
  • Creating a low-surface-energy, self-cleaning protective barrier
