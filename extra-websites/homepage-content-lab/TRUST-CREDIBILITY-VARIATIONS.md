## 🎨 DESIGN BEST PRACTICES

### **Visual Hierarchy:**

1. **Badges & Seals:** Top-right or integrated into headers
2. **Numbers/Metrics:** Large, bold, easy to scan
3. **Testimonials:** Boxed or card format with photo
4. **Certifications:** Grid layout for multiple credentials
5. **Logos:** Subtle, grayscale unless color adds value

### **Placement Guidelines:**

**Above the Fold:**
- Primary certification badges (NABL)
- Key traction metrics (200+ installations)
- Star rating (if applicable)

**Mid-Page:**
- Detailed testimonials
- Process transparency sections
- Award showcases

**Footer:**
- Compliance badges
- Industry memberships
- Investor logos (subtle)

### **Copy Tone:**

✅ **DO:**
- Be specific (numbers, names, dates)
- Use client voices (quotes)
- Show, don't just tell
- Link to proof (test reports, case studies)

❌ **DON'T:**
- Oversell or exaggerate
- Use vague claims ("best," "leading")
- Clutter with too many badges
- Bury important credentials
