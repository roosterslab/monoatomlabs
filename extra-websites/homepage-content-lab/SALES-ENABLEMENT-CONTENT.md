## 💬 SALES SCRIPTS

### **Cold Call Script - Ready-Mix Concrete**

**Opening (15 seconds):**
```
"Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] from Monoatom Labs. We help ready-mix
producers like [Their Company] offer premium concrete products that
command 20% higher prices. Bad time, or do you have 2 minutes for a
quick question about your product line?"

[If yes, continue...]

"Great! Quick question: Are your customers asking for M50 or higher-grade
concrete for high-rise or infrastructure projects?"
```

**Discovery Questions:**
```
1. "What percentage of your business is M40/M50 concrete?"

2. "How much more do you charge for M50 vs. M30?"

3. "Are you looking to differentiate your product offering, or is
    competing on price working well?"

4. "Have you experimented with any concrete additives or admixtures
    for performance enhancement?"

5. "What if you could offer M50 performance at close to M30 cost,
    with a sustainability story? Would that interest your customers?"
```

**Pitch (60 seconds):**
```
"Here's what we've done for companies like yours:

We have a graphene-based additive called Graphacrete that transforms M30
concrete to M50 performance. Your customers get:
→ 40-50% strength increase
→ 15-20% less cement (sustainability benefit)
→ Better durability and crack resistance
→ All NABL certified

For you as the producer:
→ Premium pricing opportunity (your M30+Graphacrete beats pure M50)
→ Product differentiation in competitive market
→ Sustainability credentials (green building certifications)
→ Higher margins on enhanced product line

Real example: [Partner company] launched 'Ultra-Performance Concrete'
using our technology. They got 20% premium pricing and grew that segment
35% in Year 1.

Would it make sense to explore how this could work for [Their Company]?"
```

**Objection Handling:**
```
"Too expensive"
→ "I understand. Let me ask - what's your margin on M50 now? Because
   with Graphacrete, you can offer M50 performance with premium pricing
   that more than covers the additive cost. Most partners see 15-20%
   margin improvement. Want me to run your numbers?"

"Customers won't pay more"
→ "Great point. That's why certification matters. When we show contractors
   NABL-certified data proving M50 performance plus better durability,
   they actually prefer it over pure M50. It's not 'expensive additive,'
   it's 'premium concrete solution.' Can I send you a case study?"

"We'll wait and see"
→ "Makes sense to be cautious. How about this: We offer free trial
   batches. You produce a test batch, get it NABL tested, see the
   results yourself. Zero risk. Would that work?"

"Need to think about it"
→ "Absolutely. Let me send you our information pack with case studies,
   ROI calculator, and trial batch offer. Can I follow up [specific day]
   after you've had a chance to review?"
```

**Closing:**
```
"So here's what I propose:

Step 1: I'll send you a detailed information pack today
Step 2: You review it and run your numbers
Step 3: We schedule a 30-minute call to discuss specifics for [Company]
Step 4: If it makes sense, we do a free trial batch

Sound fair? What email should I send the materials to?"
```

### **Discovery Call Script - Construction Contractor**

**Pre-Call Research:**
```
□ Check recent/upcoming projects (website, news, LinkedIn)
□ Identify project types (high-rise, infrastructure, etc.)
□ Estimate typical concrete volumes
□ Research sustainability commitments
□ Check if they mention concrete specs publicly
```

**Opening:**
```
"Thanks for taking the call, [Name]. I know your time is valuable, so
I'll keep this focused.

I've done some homework on [Company] - saw you're working on [specific
project]. Impressive work!

Before I dive in, let me ask: What are your biggest challenges right now
with concrete performance or costs on projects like that?"

[Listen, take notes]
```

**Needs Analysis Questions:**
```
Performance:
→ "What concrete grades are you typically specifying?"
→ "Ever had issues with cracking or durability?"
→ "How important is early strength development for your timelines?"

Cost:
→ "How much of your total project cost is concrete?"
→ "What's the price difference between M30 and M50 for you?"
→ "If you could get M50 performance at M30 cost, how much would you save?"

Sustainability:
→ "Are any of your projects pursuing LEED or GRIHA certification?"
→ "Is cement reduction or carbon footprint something clients ask about?"
→ "How important is sustainability in winning bids?"

Decision-Making:
→ "Who else would be involved in evaluating a new concrete solution?"
→ "What's your typical process for trying new materials?"
→ "What would you need to see to be comfortable specifying this?"
```

**Tailored Pitch (Based on Needs):**
```
If Cost-Focused:
"Based on what you've told me, here's a concrete example:

[Project type], [volume] m³, currently specifying M50
Traditional approach: [cost]
With Graphacrete: [lower cost]
Your savings: ₹[amount]

And you're actually getting BETTER durability than straight M50. Want
me to run your exact numbers?"

If Performance-Focused:
"Sounds like durability and long-term performance are critical for
[project type]. That's exactly what Graphacrete excels at:

→ 40% better water resistance (critical for [their application])
→ Excellent crack control (reduces maintenance)
→ 20-30% longer lifespan (validated in field trials)

Plus it's NABL certified. I can send you the test reports and a case
study from a similar [project type]. Would that be helpful?"

If Sustainability-Focused:
"Perfect, because Graphacrete is the only solution I know that actually
INCREASES strength while REDUCING cement:

→ 15-20% less cement per cubic meter
→ Proportional CO₂ reduction
→ Contributes to LEED materials credits
→ Sustainability story that resonates with stakeholders

Several clients have used this to win competitive bids. Want to explore
how it could work for [their upcoming project]?"
```

**Next Steps:**
```
"Here's what I'd like to propose:

1. I'll send you a customized analysis for [specific project]
2. You review it with your team
3. We schedule a follow-up to discuss specifics
4. If it looks good, we can do a pilot batch on [small portion]

Does that work? And who else should I include in the analysis so it
addresses everyone's concerns?"
```

## 🛡️ OBJECTION HANDLING GUIDE

### **Price Objections**

**"It's too expensive"**
```
Response Framework:

1. CLARIFY: "I understand. Let me make sure we're comparing apples to
   apples. Are you comparing to the cost of M30 concrete or M50?"

2. REFRAME: "If comparing to M30, yes, there's an added cost of ₹470/m³.
   But if comparing to M50, we're actually ₹430/m³ LESS expensive while
   delivering better performance."

3. VALUE: "Plus consider the cement savings (15-20%), longer lifespan
   (20-30% improvement), and reduced maintenance. Total cost of ownership
   is significantly lower."

4. PROOF: "Would it help to see a detailed cost-benefit analysis with
   real project numbers?"
```

**"We can't afford it right now"**
```
Response:

"I completely understand budget constraints. A few questions:

1. What's your budget allocated for concrete on [project]?
2. Are you facing penalties for project delays? (Graphacrete accelerates
   early strength = faster formwork removal)
3. Are you concerned about future maintenance costs?

The reason I ask is because Graphacrete often pays for itself through:
→ Schedule acceleration (earlier revenue/occupancy)
→ Reduced future maintenance
→ Better durability (fewer costly repairs)

Would it make sense to evaluate just for your next high-value project
where these benefits would be most valuable?"
```

**"Cheapest bid wins, price is everything"**
```
Response:

"I hear that a lot, and you're right that price matters. But let me ask:

When you submit bids, can you ever win on:
→ Better performance specifications?
→ Longer warranty periods?
→ Faster completion times?
→ Sustainability credentials?

Because Graphacrete gives you all of those. It's not about bidding higher,
it's about bidding smarter with better specifications that clients value.

Example: [Contractor X] started offering '50-year warranty concrete' using
Graphacrete. Higher bid price, but won [X]% more projects because clients
valued the warranty.

Want to see how they did it?"
```

```
→ Sustainability credentials provide competitive advantage?

projects, not a replacement for your standard work.

Think of it as a premium option in your portfolio. You keep your current
supplier for regular work, use Graphacrete when performance really matters.

Would that approach make more sense?"
```
