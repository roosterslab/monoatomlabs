## Section 7: Break-Even Analysis

### When Does Ceraphene Pay for Itself?

**Formula:**
```
Break-Even Point (months) = Initial Investment / Monthly Savings

Where Monthly Savings =
  (Coating cost difference / Durability in months) +
  (Annual maintenance savings / 12)
```

**Example Calculation:**
```
Single Vehicle:
- Initial investment: ₹8,000
- Coating advantage: (₹18,000 - ₹8,000) / 42 months = ₹238/month
- Maintenance savings: ₹2,880 / 12 = ₹240/month
- Total monthly savings: ₹478/month

Break-even = ₹8,000 / ₹478 = 16.7 months

After 17 months, you're in profit compared to premium alternatives.
```
