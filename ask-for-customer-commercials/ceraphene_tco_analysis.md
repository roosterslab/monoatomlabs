## Section 1: TCO Methodology & Assumptions

### What is Total Cost of Ownership?

Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) is a comprehensive financial analysis that includes:

**Direct Costs:**
- Product purchase price
- Professional application fees
- Re-application costs over time
- Maintenance products and supplies

**Indirect Costs:**
- Labor time for maintenance
- Vehicle downtime during application
- Cleaning frequency and costs
- Paint degradation and correction

**Opportunity Costs:**
- Alternative investment returns
- Business downtime
- Reduced vehicle utilization

**Hidden Benefits:**
- Extended paint life (resale value)
- Reduced insurance claims (scratch protection)
- Professional appearance (business image)
- Owner satisfaction and time savings

---

### Analysis Assumptions (Conservative Estimates)

**Vehicle Type:** Average passenger vehicle (sedan/SUV)
**Analysis Period:** 4 years (48 months)
**Application Environment:** Urban/suburban with regular use
**Maintenance:** Standard care following manufacturer recommendations

**Ceraphene Assumptions:**
- Product cost: ₹5,000 per 50ml
- Application cost: ₹3,000 (professional)
- Durability: 3.5 years average (conservative)
- Maintenance reduction: 40% (verified field data)
- Re-application: Not required in 4-year period

**Premium Competitor Assumptions:**
- Average product cost: ₹15,000 per 50ml
- Application cost: ₹3,000 (professional)
- Durability: 2.5-3 years average
- Maintenance reduction: 30%
- Re-application: Required at year 3

**Baseline (No Coating) Assumptions:**
- Washing frequency: 24 times/year × ₹300 = ₹7,200/year
- Annual waxing: 4 times × ₹1,500 = ₹6,000/year
- Paint correction: Every 2 years × ₹8,000 = ₹4,000/year average
