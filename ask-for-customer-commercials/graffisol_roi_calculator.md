## Financial Modeling Templates

### Template 1: Simple ROI Calculation

**Use this for quick estimates:**

```
Graffisol ROI Formula:

1. Baseline Annual Generation (kWh) = Plant Size (kW) × 8,760 hours × Capacity Factor

2. Additional Generation (kWh) = Baseline × 7.5%

3. Additional Annual Revenue (Rs) = Additional Generation × Tariff Rate

4. Annual Cleaning Savings (Rs) = Current Cleaning Cost × 35%

5. Total Annual Benefit (Rs) = Additional Revenue + Cleaning Savings

6. Payback Period (months) = [Graffisol Cost ÷ Total Annual Benefit] × 12

7. 25-Year ROI (%) = [(Total Annual Benefit × 25 - Graffisol Cost) ÷ Graffisol Cost] × 100
```

**Example: 500 kW Commercial Plant**

1. Baseline = 500 × 8,760 × 0.17 = 744,600 kWh/year
2. Additional = 744,600 × 0.075 = 55,845 kWh/year
3. Revenue = 55,845 × Rs 5 = Rs 2,79,225/year
4. Cleaning = Rs 1,00,000 × 0.35 = Rs 35,000/year
5. Total Benefit = Rs 2,79,225 + Rs 35,000 = Rs 3,14,225/year
6. Payback = [Rs 6,00,000 ÷ Rs 3,14,225] × 12 = 23 months
