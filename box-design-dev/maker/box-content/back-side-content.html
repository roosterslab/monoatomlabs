01 | Molecular Architecture

Graphene Matrix
Reinforcement

Standard ceramics rely on SiO2. Ceraphene embeds Graphene Nano-Platelets into the lattice. This creates a denser, less porous surface structure that physically blocks contaminants.

Core Benefit

Drastically reduced surface porosity = Superior Stain Resistance.

BOND

Covalent Bonding

Permanent adhesion to clear coat substrate.

HEAT

Heat Dissipation

Lowers surface temp to minimize water spotting.

STR

Tensile Strength

Resists micro-marring and wash-induced swirls.
