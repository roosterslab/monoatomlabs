## PRODUCT CONTEXT

### What is Graphacrete?

Graphacrete is a high-performance graphene nano-platelet additive designed for concrete that transforms standard concrete grades into high-performance materials. It creates a nano-reinforced microstructure that resists cracks, blocks water ingress, and boosts overall mechanical performance.

### Current Performance Claims

**Mechanical Performance:**
- 50% increase in compressive & flexural strength
- M30 grade concrete + Graphacrete ≈ M50 performance
- Improved crack resistance and durability
- Excellent micro-crack control

**Durability & Protection:**
- 30-45% lower permeability
- Higher chemical resistance
- Enhanced thermal dissipation

**Sustainability:**
- 15-20% cement reduction possible
- Reduced carbon footprint per cubic meter

### Technical Specifications

- **Dosage Required:** 0.05-0.10% (by weight of cement)
- **Application:** PCC, RCC & precast mixes
- **Compatibility:** Works with standard concrete mixing processes
- **Certification:** NABL certified testing

### Mechanism of Action (As Claimed)

1. **Tortuous Path for Moisture:** Graphene nano-platelets block water ingress
2. **Nano-Reinforced Microstructure:** Resists cracks and shrinkage
3. **Catalytic Support for C-S-H Gel Formation:** Reduces porosity and boosts cohesion

### Market Position

- **Price:** ₹235/L (1000L MOQ)
- **Dosage Cost:** ₹470 per cubic meter
- **Target Markets:** Construction, infrastructure, precast manufacturing
- **Competitive Advantage:** Highest strength gain at lowest dosage in industry
