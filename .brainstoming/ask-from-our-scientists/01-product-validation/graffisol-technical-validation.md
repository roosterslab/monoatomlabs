## PRODUCT CONTEXT

### What is Graffisol?

Graffisol is a next-generation graphene nanocoating designed to enhance photon absorption, conductivity, and thermal management of solar panels. It is a non-invasive coating that delivers measurable efficiency gains without any modifications to panel electrical systems.

### Current Performance Claims

**Efficiency Gains:**
- 10-12% increase in solar panel energy output (lab validated)
- 7-8% power output gain in real-world field installations
- Reduced surface reflection for improved light harvesting

**Thermal Management:**
- 5-6°C operating temperature reduction
- Minimizes heat-induced efficiency losses
- Better thermal dissipation

**Surface Protection:**
- 30-40% soiling loss reduction
- Hydrophobic & anti-soiling behavior (self-cleaning)
- >98% light transmission maintained
- Long-lasting durability under UV, dust, and moisture exposure
- Extended panel life through UV protection

**Optical Properties:**
- 99.99% transparency maintained
- 160° hydrophobicity
- 10H hardness

### Technical Specifications

- **Particle Size:** Greater than 20nm
- **Application Method:** Non-invasive spray or wipe application
- **Compatibility:** All major crystalline & thin-film solar panels
- **Transparency:** 99.99%
- **Light Transmission:** >98%
- **Durability:** Long-lasting under harsh conditions

### Mechanism of Action (As Claimed)

1. **Anti-Reflective Behavior:** Reducing surface light losses and increasing photon absorption
2. **Enhanced Thermal Dissipation:** Lowering panel operating temperature
3. **Hydrophobic & Dust-Repellent Surface:** Minimizing soiling losses

### Market Position

- **Target Markets:** Utility-scale solar plants, commercial solar, residential solar, floating solar
- **ROI:** Payback within 18 months for 1 MW plant (depending on irradiation and tariff)
- **Key Benefit:** Efficiency gain without adding panels or electrical modifications
- **Application:** Both new installations and retrofitting existing panels
