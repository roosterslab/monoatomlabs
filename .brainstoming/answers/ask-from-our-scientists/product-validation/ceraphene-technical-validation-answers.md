# CERAPHENE — Technical Validation (Draft Answers)

Scope rule: Only statements present in `source-contents/` are treated as factual product claims.

## Grounded product context (from source-contents)
- Ceraphene is a graphene-enhanced ceramic coating.
- Claims and anchors present in source materials:
  - Hardness: 9H+
  - Durability: 3–4+ years (source) / brochure comparison table also references multi-year durability range
  - Price: ₹5,000 / 50 ml
  - Competitor average reference shown: ₹15,000 / 50 ml
  - Positioning: large cost advantage vs premium ceramic coatings (requires external verification per competitor SKU)

## Q1.1: [CRITICAL] 9H+ Hardness Mechanism Validation
- Draft answer: Use “9H+ pencil hardness” only with a test method reference and reproducibility data. Mechanistically, hardness can be driven by ceramic network + crosslink density; graphene may contribute via reinforcement and barrier effects, but must be experimentally isolated (ceramic-only control).
- NEEDS INTERNAL DATA: exact formulation family, cure schedule, film thickness.
- Next validation: repeat pencil hardness per standard method + instrumented nanoindentation; include controls (no-graphene) to quantify graphene contribution.

## Q1.2: [HIGH] Scratch & Abrasion Resistance
- Draft answer: Pencil hardness is not equal to abrasion durability. Define abrasion resistance using standardized tests + real car-wash simulation.
- NEEDS INTERNAL DATA: current field feedback.
- Next validation: Taber abrasion + wash-cycle abrasion; report gloss retention vs cycles.

## Q1.3: [HIGH] Impact & Chip Resistance
- Draft answer: A thin coating is unlikely to replace PPF for chip protection; validate claims carefully and position as “scratch/chemical/UV protection” unless impact data supports more.
- NEEDS INTERNAL DATA: film thickness and modulus.
- Next validation: gravel/impact tests on coated panels; inspect for cracking/delamination.

## Q2.1: [CRITICAL] Chemical Resistance Validation
- Draft answer: Build a chemical resistance matrix relevant to automotive/marine/industrial contaminants and cleaning agents. Claims must be tied to dwell time and concentration.
- NEEDS INTERNAL DATA: existing spot tests.
- Next validation: standardized chemical exposure tests + visual + gloss + contact angle retention; document incompatible chemicals.

## Q2.2: [CRITICAL] UV Stability & Outdoor Durability
- Draft answer: Multi-year durability claims require accelerated weathering + real-world cohort tracking. Define failure modes (yellowing, gloss loss, hydrophobic decay).
- NEEDS INTERNAL DATA: any outdoor exposure panels.
- Next validation: accelerated UV/weathering + periodic performance measures; establish correlation to field aging.

## Q2.3: [HIGH] Temperature Resistance
- Draft answer: Do not claim high-temperature limits unless source-contents provides them. Validate via thermal aging with adhesion + gloss checks.
- NEEDS INTERNAL DATA: binder chemistry and Tg.
- Next validation: thermal cycling and heat soak on representative substrates.

## Q3.1: [CRITICAL] Hydrophobic Mechanism & Stability
- Draft answer: “Hydrophobic” should be defined by contact angle + hysteresis + roll-off. Stability is as important as initial value.
- NEEDS INTERNAL DATA: initial and aged contact angle measurements.
- Next validation: measure contact angle (initial + after abrasion/UV/chemicals) and define a minimum spec.

## Q3.2: [HIGH] Anti-Static Properties
- Draft answer: Anti-static is measurable (surface resistivity / charge decay). Claim only if measured and stable after wear.
- NEEDS INTERNAL DATA: any resistivity tests.
- Next validation: surface resistivity testing before/after aging; clarify whether “anti-static” is practical vs marketing.

## Q4.1: [CRITICAL] Adhesion Mechanism & Strength
- Draft answer: Adhesion depends on surface prep, chemistry, and cure. For customer safety, publish prep SOP and adhesion test results.
- NEEDS INTERNAL DATA: prep requirements and failure reports.
- Next validation: cross-hatch adhesion and pull-off tests across common automotive paint systems.

## Q4.2: [HIGH] Substrate Compatibility
- Draft answer: Validate compatibility with clear coats, marine paints, powder coats, and industrial coatings.
- NEEDS INTERNAL DATA: list of tested substrates.
- Next validation: compatibility matrix with pass/fail criteria (adhesion, haze, gloss, chemical resistance).

## Q5.1: [HIGH] Gloss Enhancement & Retention
- Draft answer: “Gloss” and “retention” need quantification (gloss meter readings at 60°) and retention after aging.
- NEEDS INTERNAL DATA: baseline vs coated gloss data.
- Next validation: gloss measurements pre/post + after abrasion/UV.

## Q5.2: [MEDIUM] Color Depth & Clarity
- Draft answer: Quantify via colorimetry (ΔE) and DOI/haze metrics; avoid subjective claims.
- NEEDS INTERNAL DATA: current photo documentation.
- Next validation: color and DOI measurement plan.

## Q6.1: [HIGH] Application Method Optimization
- Draft answer: Application is a major driver of outcomes; define training, cure conditions, and QC checks.
- NEEDS INTERNAL DATA: current SOP and rework rates.
- Next validation: applicator repeatability study; define acceptable thickness window.

## Q6.2: [MEDIUM] Cure Time & Conditions
- Draft answer: Cure schedule must be validated for different ambient conditions.
- NEEDS INTERNAL DATA: current cure-time guidance.
- Next validation: cure kinetics study vs temperature/humidity.

## Q7.1: [HIGH] Graphene Role & Contribution
- Draft answer: Demonstrate graphene contribution by A/B testing against ceramic-only baseline: hardness, abrasion, hydrophobic retention, and barrier properties.
- NEEDS INTERNAL DATA: control formulation.
- Next validation: publish “incremental benefit of graphene” charts with uncertainty bands.

## Q7.2: [MEDIUM] Cost Reduction Opportunities
