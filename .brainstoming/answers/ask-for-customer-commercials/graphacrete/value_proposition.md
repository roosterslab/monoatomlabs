# Graphacrete — Value Proposition (customer-facing, source-contents grounded)

## Problem
Construction projects often need higher-grade performance (strength + durability + water resistance). Buying higher-grade concrete can increase cost.

## Solution
Graphacrete is a graphene nano-platelet additive for cementitious mixes.

## What it delivers (claims present in source-contents)
- Compressive strength gain: 40–50%.
- Cement saving potential: 15–20%.
- Water resistance improvement: 30–45%.
- Dosage: 0.05–0.10% (brochure).
- Mix compatibility: PCC, RCC, precast.

## How it works (described mechanism)
- Creates a moisture “tortuous path” to reduce water ingress.
- Nano-reinforced microstructure resists cracks and shrinkage.
- Supports C‑S‑H gel formation, reducing porosity.

## Simple economic proof point (example present in source-contents)
- Product price: ₹235/L (MOQ 1000L).
- Example: 2L per 1 m³ → ₹470/m³ additive cost.
- Example costs: M30 1 m³ ≈ ₹4,050; M50 1 m³ ≈ ₹4,950.
- Stated cost benefit: ≈ ₹430/m³ when achieving M50-like performance via M30 + Graphacrete.

## Where it fits best
- High-rise & commercial construction
- Infrastructure (roads, bridges, dams)
- Precast
- Marine/coastal structures
