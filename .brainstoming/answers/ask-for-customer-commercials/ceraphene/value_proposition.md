# Ceraphene — Value Proposition (customer-facing, source-contents grounded)

## Problem
Premium ceramic coatings are expensive, and paint protection needs durability, hydrophobicity, and resistance to UV/chemicals.

## Solution
Ceraphene is a graphene-enhanced ceramic coating.

## What it delivers (claims present in source-contents)
- Hardness: 9H+
- Durability: 3–4+ years
- Enhanced heat & UV durability
- Hydrophobic surface; anti-static & stain-resistant barrier (brochure text)

## How it works (described mechanism)
- Graphene nano-platelets embedded in ceramic matrix.
- Dense chemically bonded protective layer.
- Reduced surface porosity lowers contaminant adhesion.

## Price/value proof point present
- Ceraphene: ₹5,000 / 50 ml
- Competitor average: ₹15,000 / 50 ml
- Claim: up to 60–70% lower cost for similar durability.

## Applications
Automotive paint, two-wheelers, commercial vehicles, marine painted surfaces, façades, industrial enclosures.
