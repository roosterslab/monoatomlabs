# Graffisol — Value Proposition (customer-facing, source-contents grounded)

## Problem
Solar generation is reduced by reflection losses, heat-induced efficiency losses, and soiling (dust).

## Solution
Graffisol is a transparent graphene nanocoating applied non-invasively to solar panels.

## What it delivers (claims present in source-contents)
- Power output gain: 7–8% (brochure table).
- Energy output gain: 10–12% (company profile).
- Operating temperature reduction: 5–6°C.
- Soiling loss reduction: 30–40%.
- Light transmission: >98%.

Website listing also states:
- Transparency 99.99%, hydrophobicity 160°, hardness 10H.

## ROI statement present in source-contents
- For a 1 MW plant, payback typically within 18 months depending on irradiation and tariff.

## How it works (described mechanism)
- Anti-reflective behavior reduces surface light losses.
- Enhanced thermal dissipation reduces operating temperature.
- Hydrophobic dust-repellent surface reduces soiling.
