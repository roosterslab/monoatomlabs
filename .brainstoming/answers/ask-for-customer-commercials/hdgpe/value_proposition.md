# HD-G-PE — Value Proposition (customer-facing, source-contents grounded)

## Problem
HDPE pipes/films/packaging and extruded products can fail due to cracking, insufficient mechanical properties, and UV exposure. Improving performance often increases cost.

## Solution
HD-G-PE is a graphene-reinforced polymer enhancer for HDPE applications.

## Claims present in source-contents
- +30% improvement in tensile & flexural strength.
- 20× improvement in elongation strength.
- +20% increase in product lifespan and durability.
- Reduced crack propagation and improved UV stability.

## Applications
- HDPE pipes
- Films and packaging
- Extrusion applications
