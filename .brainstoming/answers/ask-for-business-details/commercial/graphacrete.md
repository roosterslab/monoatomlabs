### Product identity & positioning
- Product: Graphacrete (graphene concrete additive / high-performance additive for construction materials).
- Positioning: upgrades concrete performance at low dosage; emphasizes strength gain + durability + reduced permeability + cement reduction.

### How it works (mechanism as described)
- Graphene nano platelets dispersed in cementitious matrix.
- Creates a tortuous path for moisture, reducing water ingress.
- Nano-reinforced microstructure resists cracks and shrinkage.
- Supports C‑S‑H gel formation, reducing porosity and improving cohesion.

### Key claims/specs
- Dosage required: 0.05–0.10% (brochure).
- Cement saving: 15–20% (brochure).
- Water resistance improvement: 30–45% (brochure).
- Compressive strength gain: 40–50% (brochure).
- Micro-crack control: “Excellent” (brochure table wording).
- Application compatibility: PCC, RCC, precast mixes (brochure).
