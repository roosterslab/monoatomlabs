## 📈 Market Opportunity Summary

### Commercial Products (Ready to Scale)

**Ceraphene** - Ceramic Coating Market
- Market: Automotive, marine, industrial coatings
- Competitive advantage: 60-70% cost savings vs. premium brands
- Pricing: ₹5,000/50ml vs. ₹12,000-20,000 for competitors
- Duration: 3-4+ years protection
- **Status:** Revenue generating, ready for scale

**Graffisol** - Solar Enhancement Market
- Market: $3B+ solar efficiency enhancement
- Performance: 7-8% real-world power output gain
- ROI: 18-month payback typical
- Applications: Utility, commercial, residential solar
- **Status:** Revenue generating, field-validated

**Graphacrete** - Concrete Additives Market
- Market: Construction and infrastructure
- Performance: 40-50% strength gain, 15-20% cement reduction
- Pricing: ₹235/L, saves ₹430/m³ vs. standard M50
- Certification: NABL certified
- **Status:** Revenue generating, proven performance

**HD-G-PE** - Polymer Enhancement Market
- Market: HDPE pipes, films, packaging
- Performance: 30% strength gain, 20× elongation improvement
- Applications: Pipes, films, packaging, extrusion
- **Status:** Revenue generating, multiple applications

### Pipeline Products (High Potential)

**Armophene** - Body Armor Market
- Market: $3B+ global (Military $1.5B, LE $800M, Civilian $700M)
- Advantage: 30-50% lighter than Kevlar
- Timeline: 36+ months to commercialization
- **Status:** Lab testing complete, ballistic testing in progress

**Graphosite** - Advanced Composites Market
- Market: $35B+ advanced composites
- Advantage: 30-50% strength improvement, superior toughness
- Applications: Aerospace, automotive, wind energy
- Timeline: 24-36 months to initial commercialization
- **Status:** Lab-scale complete, pilot scale in progress

**Graphyre** - Tire Technology Market
- Market: $180B+ global tire market
- Performance: +30-50% strength, -15-25% rolling resistance
- Applications: EV, commercial, performance, heavy equipment
- Timeline: 24-36 months via partnerships
- **Status:** Lab validation, partnership discussions

**Rustene** - Anti-Corrosion Market
- Market: $30B+ anti-corrosion coatings
- Advantage: 2-3× longer service life, ultra-thin coating
- Applications: Marine, infrastructure, industrial
- Timeline: 18-36 months
- **Status:** Lab testing complete, pilot scale development

**Thermaphene** - Heating Solutions Market
- Market: $23B+ (Heated apparel $8B, Automotive $7B, Building $6B)
- Advantage: 30-50% lower power, ultra-thin, flexible
- Applications: Apparel, automotive, building, medical
- Timeline: 18-24 months (apparel), 24-36 months (other)
- **Status:** Lab-scale prototypes, partnership discussions
