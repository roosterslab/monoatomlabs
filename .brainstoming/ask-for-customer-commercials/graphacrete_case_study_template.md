**Step 2: Section Completion**
Complete each section thoroughly, using actual project data. Replace all [bracketed placeholders] with specific information.

**Step 3: Customer Review**

**Step 4: Final Editing**

**Step 5: Approval & Publication**

### Customization by Project Type

**For High-Rise Residential:**
Emphasize: basement waterproofing, crack control, sustainability certifications, resident satisfaction

**For Infrastructure:**
Emphasize: durability, lifecycle costs, load capacity, reduced maintenance, extended service life

**For Precast:**
Emphasize: consistency, product differentiation, quality improvements, cost optimization, competitive advantage

**For Marine/Coastal:**

---

### Content Guidelines

**Quantify Everything:**
Use specific numbers for all claims. Replace qualitative statements with quantitative data wherever possible.

**Be Credible:**
Include third-party validation, certifications, and independent test results. Avoid unsubstantiated superlatives.

**Tell a Story:**
Create a narrative arc: challenge → solution → transformation. Make it compelling and relatable.

**Show, Don't Just Tell:**
Include photos, charts, graphs, and visual data. Reference specific test reports and documentation.

**Balance Technical & Business:**
Provide enough technical detail for engineers while remaining accessible to business decision-makers.

**Customer-Centric:**
Focus on customer challenges, perspectives, and outcomes. Let their voice come through in testimonials.
