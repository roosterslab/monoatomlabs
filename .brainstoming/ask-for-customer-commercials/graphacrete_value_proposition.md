## HOW IT WORKS

### Three-Mechanism Performance Enhancement

**1. Tortuous Path for Moisture**
Graphene nano-platelets create a complex path that blocks water ingress, dramatically improving waterproofing and durability.

**2. Nano-Reinforced Microstructure**
Nano-platelets act as microscopic reinforcement, resisting cracks and shrinkage while enhancing overall structural integrity.

**3. Catalytic C-S-H Gel Formation**
Graphene surfaces support calcium-silicate-hydrate gel formation—the primary binding phase in concrete—reducing porosity and boosting strength.
