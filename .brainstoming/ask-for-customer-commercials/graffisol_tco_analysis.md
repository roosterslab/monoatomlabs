# Graffisol Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) Analysis
## Complete 25-Year Lifecycle Financial Analysis for Solar Installations

### Executive Summary

This comprehensive Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) analysis demonstrates how Graffisol graphene nanocoating fundamentally improves the economics of solar installations across their entire 25-year lifecycle.

**Key Findings:**
- **Lower LCOE (Levelized Cost of Energy)**: 6-8% reduction in lifecycle energy costs
- **Higher IRR (Internal Rate of Return)**: 1.5-2.5% improvement in project IRR
- **Faster Payback**: 18-24 months vs. 5-7 years for solar plant itself
- **Reduced O&M Costs**: 30-40% lower maintenance over plant lifetime
- **Extended Asset Life**: UV protection adds 2-3 years of productive operation
- **Improved NPV**: 8-12% higher Net Present Value over 25 years

---

## TCO Framework: Understanding the Complete Picture

### Traditional Solar Plant TCO Components

**Capital Expenditure (CapEx):**
1. Panel procurement
2. Balance of System (BOS) - inverters, mounting structures, wiring
3. Land acquisition/lease
4. Civil works and infrastructure
5. Grid connectivity
6. Development and financing costs

**Operating Expenditure (OpEx):**
1. Cleaning and maintenance
2. Inverter replacement (typically Year 10-12)
3. Insurance and property taxes
4. Security and site management
5. Performance monitoring
6. Administrative overhead

**Performance Variables:**
1. Capacity factor (15-22% typical in India)
2. Annual degradation (0.5-0.8% per year)
3. Soiling losses (4-8% annual average)
4. Temperature losses (2-4% in hot climates)
5. System availability (95-98%)

**Graffisol Impact**: Reduces OpEx, improves performance variables, extends asset life - all contributing to superior TCO.

## LCOE (Levelized Cost of Energy) Deep Dive

### LCOE Calculation Methodology

**LCOE Formula:**
```
LCOE = Sum of Discounted Lifecycle Costs ÷ Sum of Discounted Lifecycle Energy Production
```

**Components:**
- All capital costs (Year 0)
- All operating costs (Years 1-25)
- All maintenance costs (Years 1-25)
- All replacement costs (e.g., inverters)
- Total energy produced (kWh) over 25 years
- Discount rate: 8%

---

### LCOE Comparison: Standard vs. Graffisol

#### Standard 1 MW Plant LCOE

**Costs (Present Value @ 8%):**
- CapEx: Rs 4,40,00,000 (Year 0)
- OpEx (25 years PV): Rs 74,20,000
- Maintenance (25 years PV): Rs 42,35,000
- Inverter Replacement (Year 11 PV): Rs 5,10,000
- **Total PV Costs**: Rs 5,61,65,000

**Energy Production (Present Value @ 8%):**
- Total 25-Year Generation: 36,942,000 kWh (nominal)
- Discounted Generation: 18,865,000 kWh equivalent (PV)

**LCOE Calculation:**
```
LCOE = Rs 5,61,65,000 ÷ 18,865,000 kWh = Rs 2.98/kWh
```

#### Graffisol 1 MW Plant LCOE

**Costs (Present Value @ 8%):**
- CapEx: Rs 4,50,00,000 (Year 0) - includes Graffisol


**LCOE Improvement: Rs 0.23/kWh (-7.7%)**
