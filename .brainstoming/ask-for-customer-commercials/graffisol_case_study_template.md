## Example: Completed Mini Case Study

### Utility Solar Plant in Gujarat Achieves 7.8% Energy Gain with Graffisol

**Customer:** [Anonymous], 1 MW ground-mounted solar plant, Gandhinagar, Gujarat

**Challenge:** High dust and extreme summer temperatures (45°C+) caused 8% soiling losses and required cleaning 3x per month at Rs 15,000 per cleaning. Annual cleaning costs exceeded Rs 5 lakhs.

**Solution:** Applied Graffisol in March 2025 to entire 1 MW installation (2,500 panels, 6,250 m²).

**Results (12-Month Performance):**
- **Energy Gain:** 7.8% average (+117,000 kWh annually)
- **Additional Revenue:** Rs 5.85 lakhs/year (at Rs 5/kWh)
- **Temperature Reduction:** 5.8°C average, 6.5°C peak summer
- **Cleaning Frequency:** 3x/month reduced to 1x/month
- **Cleaning Cost Savings:** Rs 3.6 lakhs/year (64% reduction)
- **Total Annual Benefit:** Rs 9.45 lakhs
- **Payback Achieved:** 16 months
- **25-Year Projected Value:** Rs 2.36 Crores

**Customer Quote:**
> "Graffisol has transformed our plant economics. The 7.8% energy gain alone justified the investment, but the dramatic reduction in cleaning needs has been equally valuable. In Gujarat's dusty environment, our panels stay cleaner longer, and the monsoon rains literally wash them clean. Best ROI improvement we've made since commissioning."
> — Plant Manager
