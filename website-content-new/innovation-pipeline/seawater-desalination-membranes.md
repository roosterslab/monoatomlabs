# Seawater Desalination Membranes

## Category
Field-Scale Pilot Trials

## Overview
Ultra-thin graphene membranes delivering high-flux, low-energy seawater desalination and industrial water purification.

## Key Features
- Ultra-thin graphene membrane design
- High-flux performance
- Low-energy consumption
- Efficient seawater desalination
- Industrial water purification capability

## Applications
- Seawater desalination plants
- Industrial water treatment
- Municipal water purification
- Coastal water supply systems
- Water scarcity solutions
