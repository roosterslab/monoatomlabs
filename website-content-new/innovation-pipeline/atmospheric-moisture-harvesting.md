# Atmospheric Moisture Harvesting (Air-to-Water)

## Category
Field-Scale Pilot Trials

## Overview
Graphene membranes engineered to harvest atmospheric moisture and convert air into potable water efficiently.

## Key Features
- Atmospheric moisture capture technology
- Graphene membrane engineering
- Efficient air-to-water conversion
- Potable water generation
- Sustainable water production

## Applications
- Arid and desert regions
- Emergency water supply
- Off-grid communities
- Disaster relief operations
- Sustainable water infrastructure

Field-Scale Pilot Trials
