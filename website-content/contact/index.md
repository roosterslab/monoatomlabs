## Frequently Asked Questions

### Quick Answers to Common Questions

**Q: How quickly can I expect a response to my inquiry?**
A: We typically respond to all inquiries within 24 business hours. For urgent matters, please call us directly during business hours.

**Q: Can I visit your facilities?**
A: Yes, we welcome facility visits by appointment. Please contact us in advance to schedule your visit and specify your areas of interest.

**Q: Do you provide product samples?**
A: Sample availability depends on the product and application. Contact our technical team to discuss your specific requirements and we'll work to accommodate sample requests for qualified projects.

**Q: How do I become a distribution partner?**
A: Please contact our sales team (sales@monoatomlabs.com) with information about your company, market coverage, and relevant experience. We'll evaluate partnership opportunities based on market fit and capabilities.

**Q: Can you develop custom solutions for specific applications?**
A: Yes, we work with partners on custom material development and application-specific optimization. Contact our R&D team to discuss your requirements.

**Q: What information should I include in my technical inquiry?**
A: Please provide as much detail as possible about your application, performance requirements, current materials/methods, volume requirements, and timeline. This helps us provide more accurate and useful responses.

**Q: Do you offer training and technical support?**
A: Yes, comprehensive technical support, training, and implementation assistance are provided to partners and customers. Specific offerings depend on the partnership model and product.

**Q: How do I inquire about partnership opportunities?**
A: Contact our partnership team (partnerships@monoatomlabs.com) or use the contact form selecting "Partnership Opportunity." Include information about your organization, capabilities, and areas of interest.
