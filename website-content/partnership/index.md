## Model 1: JV-SPV (Joint Venture - Special Purpose Vehicle)

### Co-Create, Co-Develop, Co-Commercialize

Partner with Monoatom Labs through Joint Ventures via Special Purpose Vehicles to co-develop, manufacture, and commercialize breakthrough graphene products.

#### How It Works

**Structure:**
- Formation of dedicated Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV)
- Joint ownership and governance
- Shared investment and resources
- Co-branded product development
- Joint commercialization strategy

**What We Bring:**
- Proprietary graphene technology & process IP
- R&D know-how and formulation expertise
- Technical team support and scale-up guidance
- Ongoing innovation and product development
- Quality control and characterization protocols

**What Partners Bring:**
- Manufacturing infrastructure and capacity
- Market access & established customer relationships
- Capital investment and operational execution
- Industry expertise and distribution networks
- Regulatory compliance and market knowledge

**Shared Benefits:**

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Partnership FAQs

**Q: What industries do you prioritize for partnerships?**
We work across construction, solar, automotive, polymers, and advanced materials. Priority goes to partners with established manufacturing and market access.

**Q: How long does partnership formation take?**
Licensing agreements typically finalize in 1-3 months. JV-SPV structures take 3-6 months including due diligence and legal formation.

**Q: What level of exclusivity is offered?**
Both exclusive and non-exclusive options available based on territory, application, and commitment levels.

**Q: What if we want to pilot the technology first?**
We offer pilot programs and trial batches to validate performance before full partnership commitment.

**Q: Do you provide marketing support?**
Yes, we provide technical marketing materials, performance data, competitive analyses, and customer education resources.

**Q: What about IP protection?**
All partnerships include clear IP ownership terms, confidentiality agreements, and protection mechanisms for both parties.

**Q: Can we customize formulations?**
Yes, we work with partners to optimize formulations for specific applications and manufacturing processes.

**Q: What ongoing support is provided?**
Technical support, troubleshooting, process optimization, quality control, and continuous innovation are all included.
