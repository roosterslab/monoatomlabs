## Facility 01: Nanomaterials R&D Facility

### Overview

Dedicated facility focused on graphene processing, dispersion technologies, and high-purity nano-additive development. This is where breakthrough formulations are created, optimized, and prepared for scale-up.

### Core Focus Areas

#### Graphene Processing
- Synthesis and production of high-quality graphene
- Functionalization and surface modification
- Purity optimization and quality control
- Batch-to-batch consistency development

#### Dispersion Technologies
- Uniform nano-particle dispersion in various matrices
- Stability optimization for different applications
- Solvent and carrier system development
- Scale-up process development

#### High-Purity Nano-Additive Development
- Formulation of application-specific additives
- Performance optimization
- Compatibility testing with host materials
- Quality assurance protocols

### Advanced Characterization Capabilities

#### Structural Characterization
- **SEM (Scanning Electron Microscopy)** - Surface morphology and structure analysis
- **TEM (Transmission Electron Microscopy)** - Atomic-scale structure visualization
- **Raman Spectroscopy** - Graphene quality and layer analysis
- **XRD (X-Ray Diffraction)** - Crystal structure analysis

#### Surface Analysis
- **Surface Chemistry Modification** - Functional group attachment and optimization
- **Surface Area Measurement** - BET analysis for specific surface area
- **Zeta Potential Analysis** - Dispersion stability characterization
- **Contact Angle Measurement** - Wettability and surface energy

#### Physical Properties
- **Particle Size Analysis** - Distribution and consistency measurement
- **Bulk Density Testing** - Material handling properties
- **Thickness Measurement** - Layer characterization
- **Purity Analysis** - Chemical composition verification

### Pilot-Scale Capabilities

#### Dispersion & Functionalization Units
- Pilot-scale mixing and dispersion equipment
- Temperature-controlled reaction systems
- Continuous and batch processing options
- Scale-up optimization platforms

#### Quality Control Systems
- In-process monitoring and control
- Batch consistency verification
- Specification compliance testing
- Documentation and traceability

## Facility 02: Application & Testing Facility

### Overview

Designed for real-industry testing across coatings, concrete, composites, energy systems, and bio-chemical applications. This facility validates performance claims and ensures products meet demanding industrial standards.

### Testing Capabilities by Sector

#### Construction Materials Testing
- **Mechanical Testing**
  - Compressive strength testing (concrete, mortar)
  - Flexural strength analysis
  - Tensile testing
  - Impact resistance
- **Durability Testing**
  - Water permeability
  - Chemical resistance
  - Freeze-thaw cycling
  - Accelerated weathering
- **Specialized Analysis**
  - Crack propagation studies
  - Microstructure analysis
  - Long-term performance monitoring

#### Coatings & Surface Treatment Testing
- **Hardness Testing** - 9H pencil hardness and beyond
- **Adhesion Testing** - Cross-hatch and pull-off tests
- **Environmental Resistance**
  - UV exposure chambers
  - Salt spray testing
  - Chemical resistance
- **Surface Properties**
  - Hydrophobicity measurement (contact angle)
  - Gloss and color retention
  - Anti-soiling performance

#### Energy Systems Testing
- **Solar Panel Performance**
  - Efficiency measurement systems
  - Temperature monitoring
  - Light transmission analysis
  - Long-term outdoor exposure
- **Battery Testing**
  - Charge-discharge cycling
  - Power density measurement
  - Thermal safety analysis
  - Lifecycle testing

#### Polymer & Composite Testing
- **Mechanical Properties**
  - Tensile strength and elongation
  - Flexural properties
  - Impact resistance
  - Fatigue testing
- **Environmental Testing**
  - UV stability
  - Thermal aging
  - Chemical resistance
  - Weathering simulation

### Advanced Testing Equipment

#### Mechanical & Durability Testing
- Universal Testing Machines (UTM)
- Compression testing equipment
- Impact testers
- Fatigue and cyclic loading systems
- Environmental chambers

#### Thermal & Electrical Testing
- **Thermal Conductivity Labs**
  - Thermal analysis equipment (DSC, TGA)

  - Temperature cycling chambers
- **Electrical Conductivity Labs**
  - Resistivity measurement
  - Electrochemical analysis
  - Battery testing stations

#### Prototype Fabrication
- Sample preparation equipment
- Coating application systems
- Mixing and dispersion tools
- Curing and processing equipment

#### Field-Simulation Setups
- Outdoor exposure racks
- Accelerated weathering chambers
- Real-condition simulation systems
- Long-term monitoring stations
