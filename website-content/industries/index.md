## Construction & Infrastructure

### Building the Future with High-Performance Materials

**Industry Challenges We Address:**
- Increasing strength requirements without proportional cost increases
- Cement reduction for sustainability and carbon footprint goals
- Durability and crack resistance in harsh environments
- Water resistance and chemical protection needs
- Long-term structural integrity and lifecycle costs

**Our Solutions:**

#### Graphacrete - High-Performance Concrete Additive
Transform standard concrete into high-performance material with 40-50% strength gains, 15-20% cement reduction, and superior durability.

**Key Performance Metrics:**
- 40-50% compressive strength increase at 0.05-0.10% dosage
- 30-45% improvement in water resistance
- 15-20% cement savings = reduced CO₂ footprint
- Excellent micro-crack control and durability
- NABL certified: M30 + Graphacrete ≈ M50 performance

**Applications:**
- High-rise and commercial construction
- Infrastructure projects: roads, bridges, dams
- Precast concrete manufacturing
- Marine and coastal structures
- Sustainable housing projects
- Industrial flooring and pavements

**Economic Impact:**
- ₹430/m³ cost savings vs. upgrading concrete grade
- Extended structure lifespan reducing maintenance
- Reduced material consumption and carbon credits
- Premium pricing opportunity for enhanced products

## Solar & Clean Energy

### Maximizing Energy Output and Efficiency

**Industry Challenges We Address:**
- Maximizing energy output from installed capacity
- Reducing operating temperatures in hot climates
- Minimizing soiling and maintenance requirements
- Extending panel lifespan and protecting surfaces
- Improving ROI without hardware replacement

**Our Solutions:**

#### Graffisol - Solar Panel Enhancement Coating
Graphene nanocoating that increases energy output by 7-8% through enhanced photon absorption, thermal management, and self-cleaning properties.

**Key Performance Metrics:**
- 7-8% real-world power output increase (field validated)
- 5-6°C operating temperature reduction
- 30-40% reduction in soiling losses
- >98% light transmission maintained
- Extended panel lifespan through UV protection

**Applications:**
- Utility-scale solar power plants
- Commercial and industrial rooftop installations
- Residential solar systems
- Floating solar plants
- High-dust and high-temperature environments
- Panel retrofitting and efficiency upgrades

**Economic Impact:**
- 18-month payback period for 1MW installations
- Lowest cost per additional watt generated
- Reduced maintenance and cleaning frequency
- Improved LCOE (Levelized Cost of Energy)
- Extended equipment ROI

**Energy Storage Solutions:**
Graphene battery storage systems in pilot phase deliver higher power density, faster response, and extended lifecycle for grid-scale and commercial applications.

**Industry Challenges We Address:**

- Durability and scratch resistance requirements
