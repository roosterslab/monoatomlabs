## How It Works

### Advanced Nano-Enhancement Mechanism

Graffisol utilizes ultra-fine graphene nano structures uniformly dispersed within a transparent coating matrix. These enable three critical performance enhancements:

#### Anti-Reflective Behavior
Reduces surface light losses by minimizing reflection, allowing more photons to penetrate the solar panel surface and increasing overall energy absorption.

#### Enhanced Thermal Dissipation
Graphene's superior thermal conductivity lowers panel operating temperature by 5-6°C, minimizing heat-induced efficiency losses and maintaining optimal performance even in high-temperature conditions.

#### Hydrophobic & Dust-Repellent Surface
Creates a hydrophobic surface with 160° water contact angle that repels water, dust, and contaminants. This minimizes soiling losses by 30-40% and provides self-cleaning properties that reduce maintenance requirements.
