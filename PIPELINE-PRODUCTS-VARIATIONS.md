## 🚨 IMPORTANT MESSAGING GUIDELINES

### **DO:**
✅ Be transparent about development stage
✅ Use realistic timelines (Q2 2026, not "coming soon")
✅ Emphasize market need/problem being solved
✅ Highlight partnership opportunities
✅ Show connection to commercial success (4/4 products)
✅ Invite early engagement (beta testing, co-development)

### **DON'T:**
❌ Over-promise performance before validation
❌ Present pipeline as equivalent to commercial products
❌ Use vague launch dates ("soon," "next year")
❌ Ignore development risk (be honest about stage)
❌ Neglect to offer concrete engagement paths
❌ Forget to update as products progress
