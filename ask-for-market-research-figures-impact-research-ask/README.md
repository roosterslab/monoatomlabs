## Research Structure

Each product research request file contains 14 comprehensive sections:

### 1. Market Size & Opportunity
- Global and regional market data
- Market segmentation
- TAM/SAM/SOM analysis
- Growth projections

### 2. Competitive Landscape
- Direct competitor analysis
- Competitive positioning
- Market share data
- Competitive advantages validation

### 3. Customer Segments & Insights
- Primary customer groups
- Pain points and needs
- Customer acquisition metrics
- Buying behavior

### 4. Impact Metrics & Validation
- Environmental impact
- Economic impact
- Social impact
- Performance validation data

### 5. Pricing & Unit Economics
- Pricing analysis
- Unit economics
- Revenue projections
- Break-even analysis

### 6. Go-to-Market Strategy Data
- Distribution channels
- Marketing approach
- Sales strategy
- Partnership opportunities

### 7. Research & Development
- Technology validation
- Patent & IP landscape
- R&D investment requirements
- Testing protocols

### 8. Regulatory & Standards
- Certifications needed
- Testing protocols
- Compliance requirements
- Industry standards

### 9. Supply Chain & Manufacturing
- Production capacity
- Supply chain analysis
- Manufacturing economics
- Scaling requirements

### 10. Success Metrics & KPIs
- Performance benchmarks
- Market penetration targets
- Business metrics
- Product-market fit indicators

### 11. Gaps in Current Content
- Missing critical data
- Additional research needs
- Content enhancement priorities

### 12. Strategic Questions to Answer
- Market entry decisions
- Competitive strategy
- Growth strategy
- Investment & funding

### 13. Research Methodology Suggestions
- Primary research methods
- Secondary research sources
- Quantitative analysis approaches

### 14. Deliverables Requested
- Market research reports
- Financial models
- Impact assessments
- Go-to-market plans

## How to Use This Directory

### For Internal Team
1. **Review** each product's research request file
2. **Prioritize** research areas based on immediate needs
3. **Assign** research tasks to team members or external consultants
4. **Track** progress using checkboxes in each file
5. **Update** files as research data is obtained
6. **Create** summary reports in dedicated folders

### For External Research Partners
1. **Select** products to research
2. **Follow** the structured format in each file
3. **Address** checkboxed items systematically
4. **Provide** data sources and methodology
5. **Deliver** findings in the requested format

### For Investors & Partners
1. **Review** to understand our analytical approach
2. **Identify** areas where you can contribute data/insights
3. **Provide** feedback on priority research areas
4. **Use** as basis for due diligence discussions
