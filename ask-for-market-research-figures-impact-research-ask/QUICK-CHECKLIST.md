## RESEARCH RISK MITIGATION

### Risk: Incomplete or unreliable market data
**Mitigation:** Engage multiple research sources, triangulate data

### Risk: Customer unwillingness to participate in interviews
**Mitigation:** Offer incentives, leverage network, use intermediaries

### Risk: Competitive intelligence difficult to obtain
**Mitigation:** Mystery shopping, industry experts, channel interviews

### Risk: Research timeline delays
**Mitigation:** Parallel workstreams, clear milestones, weekly reviews

### Risk: Budget overruns
**Mitigation:** Phase-gate approach, fixed-price contracts, contingency buffer
