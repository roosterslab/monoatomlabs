### PRODUCT 1: GRAPHACRETE

**Category:** Construction & Infrastructure Additive
**Status:** Commercially Available
**Target Market:** Construction industry, infrastructure projects

#### Product Description
High-performance graphene nano-platelet additive for concrete that transforms standard concrete grades into high-performance materials. Creates a nano-reinforced microstructure that resists cracks, blocks water ingress, and boosts overall mechanical performance.

#### How It Works
Graphacrete utilizes graphene nano-platelets dispersed uniformly within the cementitious matrix. These create:
- **Tortuous Path for Moisture:** Blocking water ingress effectively
- **Nano-Reinforced Microstructure:** Resisting cracks and shrinkage
- **Catalytic Support for C-S-H Gel Formation:** Reducing porosity and boosting cohesion

#### Key Benefits
1. **50% increase** in compressive & flexural strength
2. **Improved crack resistance** and durability
3. **30-45% lower permeability** & higher chemical resistance
4. **15-20% cement reduction** - reduced carbon footprint per cubic meter
5. **Enhanced thermal dissipation** and greater energy savings
6. **Excellent micro-crack control**

#### Technical Specifications
- **Dosage Required:** 0.05-0.10% (by weight of cement)
- **Cement Saving:** 15-20%
- **Water Resistance Improvement:** 30-45%
- **Compressive Strength Gain:** 40-50%
- **Micro-Crack Control:** Excellent
- **Compatibility:** PCC, RCC & precast mixes

#### Performance Data (NABL Certified)
- M30 Cube + Graphacrete ≈ M50 performance
- Highest strength gain (40-50%) at lowest dosage in industry

#### Cost-Benefit Analysis
- **Product Price:** ₹235/L (1000L MOQ)
- **Dosage:** 2L solution required for 1m³ concrete = ₹470
- **Cost Comparison:**
  - M30 grade 1m³ = ₹4,050 (approx)
  - Additive cost = ₹470
  - Total = ₹4,520
  - M50 grade 1m³ = ₹4,950 (approx)
- **Cost Benefit:** ₹430/m³ savings
- **Environmental Benefit:** 15-20% cement reduction = significant CO₂ savings

#### Applications
- High-rise & commercial construction
- Infrastructure projects: Roads, bridges, dams
- Precast concrete manufacturing
- Marine and coastal structures
- Sustainable housing projects
- RCC structures
- Highway construction
- Critical infrastructure

**Status:** Commercially Available

- **Anti-Reflective Behavior:** Reducing surface light losses and increasing photon absorption
- **Enhanced Thermal Dissipation:** Lowering panel operating temperature by 5-6°C
- **Hydrophobic & Dust-Repellent Surface:** Minimizing soiling losses by 30-40%

#### Key Benefits
1. **10-12% increase** in solar panel energy output (field & lab validated)
2. **7-8% power output gain** in real-world installations
