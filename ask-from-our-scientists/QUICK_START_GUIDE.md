## ⚠️ RISK MITIGATION

### Risk: Consultants Validate Issues with Claims
**Impact:** HIGH - May need to adjust marketing
**Mitigation:** Treat as opportunity to refine, not crisis. Better to know now than face customer complaints later.

### Risk: Budget Overruns
**Impact:** MEDIUM - Could delay some workstreams
**Mitigation:** Fixed-price contracts, stage-gate approvals, 15% contingency budget

### Risk: Scale-Up Delays
**Impact:** HIGH - Could delay revenue growth
**Mitigation:** Start scale-up work in parallel with validation (Phase 1), not sequentially

### Risk: Consultant Availability
**Impact:** MEDIUM - Could delay timeline
**Mitigation:** Identify backup consultants, flexible engagement models

### Risk: IP Disputes
**Impact:** HIGH - Could lose competitive advantage
**Mitigation:** Robust NDAs, clear IP ownership in contracts, legal review
