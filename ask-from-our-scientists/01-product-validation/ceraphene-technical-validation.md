## PRODUCT CONTEXT

### What is Ceraphene?

Ceraphene is an ultra-durable graphene-enhanced ceramic coating providing industry-leading protection, hardness, and surface performance for automotive, marine, industrial, and architectural painted surfaces. It combines graphene nano-platelets with ceramic coating matrix for superior protection at 60-70% lower cost than premium ceramic coatings.

### Current Performance Claims

**Mechanical & Protection:**
- 9H+ hardness with extreme scratch resistance
- Enhanced UV, heat, and chemical durability
- 3-4+ years durability

**Surface Properties:**
- Ultra-hydrophobic surface
- Anti-static & stain-resistant barrier
- Excellent gloss and color depth
- Extends coating lifecycle by 25-40%

**Economic:**
- ₹5,000/50ml vs ₹15,000/50ml for premium competitors
- 60-70% cost advantage
- Lower cost per protection year
- Very high cost effectiveness

### Technical Specifications

- **Hardness:** 9H+ (exceeds industry standards)
- **Durability:** 3-4+ years
- **Hydrophobicity:** Excellent
- **Heat & UV Resistance:** Excellent
- **Gloss & Color Depth:** Excellent
- **Application:** Bonds directly to painted surfaces
- **Coverage:** 1 average passenger vehicle per 50ml

### Mechanism of Action (As Claimed)

1. **Formation of Dense, Chemically Bonded Protective Layer**
2. **Enhanced Resistance to Heat, UV Radiation, and Oxidation**
3. **Reduced Surface Porosity:** Limiting dust, dirt, and contaminant adhesion

### Market Position

- **Target Markets:** Automotive detailing, marine, industrial equipment, architectural coatings
- **Competitive Advantage:** Only graphene-enhanced formulation at this price point
- **Key Differentiation:** Premium performance at ceramic-only pricing
