**Question:**

   - Shear rate distribution in large vessels?
   - Heat generation and dissipation?
   - Mixing uniformity and dead zones?
   - Batch time scaling?
2. What equipment types are most suitable:
   - High-shear rotor-stator mixers (inline vs. batch)?
   - Bead mills (horizontal vs. vertical)?
   - Ultrasonic processors (batch vs. flow-through)?
   - Combination approaches?
3. What are critical process parameters to control:
   - Shear rate and energy input?

   - Residence time distribution?
