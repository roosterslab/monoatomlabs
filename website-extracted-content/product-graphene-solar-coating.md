# Graphene Solar Coating - Monoatomlabs

## Product Overview

Monoatomlabs presents a graphene-based solar surface coating designed to enhance photovoltaic panel performance.

## Key Specifications

The coating demonstrates several notable technical characteristics:

- **Efficiency Increment**: Up to 17% additional power output
- **Transparency**: 99.99%
- **Hydrophobicity**: 160°
- **Hardness Test**: 10H
- **Particle Size**: Greater than 20nm

## Product Description

The company describes this as "the world's first IP driven, graphene-based solar surface coating, delivering up to 17% more power output by maximizing energy efficiency and panel performance."

## Company Information

**Monoatomlabs** operates as the manufacturer and distributor of this advanced coating technology. The organization maintains an online presence at monoatomlabs.com, where customers can access product information and potentially place orders.
